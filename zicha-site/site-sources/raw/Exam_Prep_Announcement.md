## Study Tips

As we wind down, I think that the exam study keys are actually pretty helpful, and particularly the "key insight" sections that help to conceptually link all the problem sets. Again, these aren't there to memorize, and — and I cannot stress this enough — **this is not a "math test."** So, memorizing formulas, or stressing over this or that calculation being exactly right from the problem set is of less value than getting key concepts down and understanding how to approach such problems.

For obvious reasons (hours of time vs. 90 minutes and the need to cover all that material) the problems for the exam will be much less complicated than the problem sets.

My goal with the exam design has been / is (still finalizing) that I cover the things I really want you to know after leaving this course that you didn't know before. Thus, if I do it well (and you did your part well) we should have a somewhat "wow, look at all we learned" experience. But, in order for that to happen it's important everyone rest, and take some time to review, focus on the bits you really struggle with, and try to synthesize a bit of the value and relevance of the skills and questions there. Doing that will likely be the best guide to the exam.
