Decision-Making Processes

A unified analytical framework for understanding decision-making processes across all scales — from individual choices, through decentralized markets and centralized hierarchies, to collective choice. Along the way we build transferable “bookshelf models”: principal-agent theory, spatial models of choice, social choice theory, and game theory.
