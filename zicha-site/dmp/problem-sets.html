Decision-Making Processes · Workbook

Problem Sets

Four scaffolded problem sets, each a preview of the technical material on the in-class exam. Answer each part in writing, by filling in the table, by drawing on the sketch pad, or by uploading a picture of your own diagram. Work is saved as you go. Submit when a set is complete: the model answers are then shown beside your own so you can study the comparison on screen.

Checking where your work is saved…

How it works. Problem sets are pass/fail: you pass by making a serious attempt at every part. Collaboration is welcome — work alone or in a group workspace — but each student submits under their own name and is responsible for what they submit; collaborators are recorded with the submission.
