Institutions of Governance and Development

The foundational course of the Governance, Economics, and Development major. We learn a way of thinking — institutional analysis — by reading closely one of the seminal texts of the new institutionalism, Douglass North’s Institutions, Institutional Change and Economic Performance, and applying it each week to real problems of governance and development.

Pop reflections (“letters”) are written in class in session 2 of weeks 1–3 and 5–7 — Thursdays 29 Oct, 5 Nov, 12 Nov, 26 Nov, 3 Dec and 10 Dec. Six letters, 5% each, lowest dropped (25%). Participation is 10%, assessed continuously.

Every component must be submitted or attended to pass. An unfinished essay handed in is a grade; an essay not handed in is a fail. The dropped-lowest rule on quizzes and letters is the only accommodation built in.
